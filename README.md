# Island Sim

A browser prototype of an Anno/Tropico-style city-builder set on one tropical atoll, where terraforming is the core mechanic.

## Play

Serve the folder and open `index.html`, for example `python3 -m http.server` then http://localhost:8000. There is no build step; Three.js 0.170 loads from jsDelivr. Opening the file directly from disk will not work, because browsers block reading local textures.

Pick a graphics preset in the header: **Lo** (phones), **Hi** (refraction, bloom, grass) or **Ul** (adds ambient occlusion and tilt-shift). The choice is remembered.

## What's in it

- Three islands on one atoll ring: Palm Isle (start), Basalt Isle (mountain, stone) and Sugar Cay (farmland behind lagoon shallows).
- Raise, lower and level land. Raising costs stone from the nearest harbor; lowering returns some.
- Deep water carries boats, shallow reef blocks them but feeds fisheries. Dredge channels, build land bridges, or split islands.
- Each island has its own warehouse; boats shuttle goods to the capital. Land bridges merge warehouses.
- Houses eat fish and pay more tax with rum. Fishery, lumber camp, quarry, sugar field and distillery.

## Controls

Left-drag paints the current tool (Survey pans). Right-drag or Q/E rotates, wheel or pinch zooms, WASD pans. Hotkeys: V survey, R raise, F lower, T level, X demolish, H harbor, 1–6 buildings, B brush size, Space pause.

## Graphics

- Physically based lighting with a Preetham sky, image-based lighting from that sky, ACES tone mapping and soft 4K shadows.
- Terrain blends six texture layers (sand, grass, forest floor, rock, reef, soil) with normal maps; rock is projected triplanar on cliffs. Wet sand at the waterline, caustics on the sea floor, drifting cloud shadows.
- Water: layered normal maps, Fresnel sky reflection, depth-based colour absorption, screen-space refraction, sun glints, shoreline and reef-crest foam.
- Instanced palms, broadleaf trees, bushes, rocks and grass that sway in the wind; plaster, tile, thatch and wood buildings.
- Post-processing: bloom, GTAO ambient occlusion and tilt-shift on Ultra.

Textures in `assets/textures` are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` (run `python3 tools/gen_textures.py`; needs numpy and Pillow), except `water_normal.jpg`, which comes from CesiumJS (see `NOTICE.md`). Scanned CC0 textures from Poly Haven or ambientCG can replace any of them: keep the file names, 1024×1024, albedo in sRGB and OpenGL-style normal maps.
